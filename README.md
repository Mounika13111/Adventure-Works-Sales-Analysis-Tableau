# Adventure Works Sales Analysis Dashboard | Tableau

## Project Overview

This project uses Tableau to analyze Adventure Works sales data and explore sales performance, profitability, production costs, regional performance, product sales, and monthly trends.

## Project Objectives

- Monitor total sales, total profit, and total production cost.
- Calculate and evaluate profit margin.
- Compare sales across regions.
- Identify the top five products by sales.
- Analyze quarter-wise and month-wise sales trends.
- Compare sales and product costs over time.
- Explore results using year and region filters.

## Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Production Cost, and Profit Margin.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales distribution across regions.
- **Top Products:** Five leading products by sales.
- **Quarterly Analysis:** Sales distribution by quarter.
- **Monthly Trend:** Month-wise sales performance.
- **Sales and Cost Analysis:** Combined chart comparing monthly sales and product cost.
- **Interactive Filters:** Year and Region.

## Key Insights

- Total sales displayed: 29.36M.
- Total profit displayed: 12.08M.
- Total production cost displayed: 17.28M.
- Profit margin displayed: 41.15%.
- Australia has the highest displayed regional sales at approximately 9.06M.
- The top five product chart shows sales values ranging from approximately 1.29M to 1.37M.
- Monthly sales reach their highest displayed value in December at approximately 3.21M.
